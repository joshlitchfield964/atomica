--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FACT-CHECK NOTES CARRIED INTO THE EDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every Tesla-supplied figure on screen carries a red "CLAIMED · TESLA" stamp.
  None of them — 0.81 s, 80 kN, 250 kg of air, 2 G, <0.59 s — has been
  independently measured. The graphics never let a Tesla number look verified.
- The 27 kg/s burn rate is a PUBLIC ESTIMATE, not a Tesla figure. It is stamped
  separately in slate with a dashed border and the words "not Tesla-confirmed",
  because the film's own argument depends on not overstating it.
- Rimac Nevera (1.74 s) and Pininfarina Battista (1.79 s) are shown as
  independently verified, which is what makes the comparison meaningful.
- "No FAA certificate in federal records" is presented as an absence of record,
  never as a refusal or denial by the FAA.
- Lilium's collapse is stated factually. No mockery; a failed company's staff
  are not the story.
- The narration's machine transcript misheard several proper nouns. On screen
  the film uses the correct spellings: Rimac Nevera, Pininfarina Battista,
  Nardò, Jim Dukhovny, Alef Aeronautics, Joby Aviation, XPeng, Evel Knievel.
